What to do

Five steps for you

Step 1

Adopt Intermittent Fasting and Eliminate Refined Starches

Why: Lowering chronically elevated insulin is mandatory to unlock visceral abdominal fat, reverse prediabetes, and eliminate brain fog.

How: Follow a 16:8 time-restricted feeding schedule, completely cut sugary beverages and refined grains, and build meals around whole proteins, healthy fats, and vegetables.

Step 2

Establish a Non-Negotiable 7-Hour Sleep and Screen Curfew

Why: Sleeping only 5.5 hours elevates cortisol, worsens insulin resistance, and accelerates cognitive burnout.

How: Turn off all digital devices 60 minutes before bedtime, keep the bedroom completely dark and cool, and protect a 7 to 8-hour sleep window nightly.

Step 3

Build a 6-Month Liquid Emergency Fund and Freeze Debt

Why: A 45% EMI paired with small savings creates acute panic that ruins decision-making during corporate restructuring.

How: Audit monthly bank statements line-by-line, halt all non-essential consumer spending, and redirect cash into liquid bank deposits until you hold 6 months of expenses.

Step 4

Upgrade from Administrative Coordinator to AI Fleet Manager

Why: Traditional project management is rapidly automated, whereas managing autonomous AI agents makes you indispensable.

How: Spend 5 focused hours each week learning enterprise automation workflows and generative tools, deploying them directly to streamline team delivery.

Step 5

Practice Stoic Detachment and Witness Awareness at Work

Why: Catastrophic overthinking over office politics drains nervous energy and increases physical cardiovascular risk.

How: Focus exclusively on daily choices within your direct control, viewing corporate chaos with calm emotional distance rather than taking pressure personally.

Watch out

Mistakes to avoid

⛔ Relying on exercise alone to reverse prediabetes and lose belly fat

Exercise burns few calories relative to basal metabolism and triggers rebound hunger while insulin remains too high to access stored body fat.

⛔ Taking on additional consumer debt or lifestyle upgrades to cope with stress

Compounding interest on lifestyle liabilities drains cash flow and deepens debt slavery during periods of workplace instability.

⛔ Hoping corporate tenure and loyalty will protect your middle-management role from AI

Companies ruthlessly cut administrative layers to protect margins, prioritizing automation and lean operational efficiency over past service.

⛔ Lying awake at night catastrophizing about future job cuts instead of sleeping

Imaginary worry triggers adrenaline surges and destroys sleep quality without producing a single practical step toward career security.
