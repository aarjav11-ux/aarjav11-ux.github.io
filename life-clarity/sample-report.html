What is really going on

The Illusion of Noble Sacrifice

You believe you are being a loving and dutiful child by considering their marriage timeline and carrying financial expectations. In reality, this duty is rooted in guilt and fear of conflict rather than genuine love or clarity. Sacrificing your life to preserve family social standing only enables traditional vanity while breeding silent, lifelong bitterness.

The Category Error of Career Escapism

You assume that quitting IT to write or teach will automatically dissolve the heavy emptiness you experience every day. A career cannot heal an internal psychological void, and without self-knowledge, the same restlessness will infect your teaching or writing. Real clarity requires looking at the unexamined inner seeker first rather than expecting an external job title to provide meaning.

Numbing Through the Screen

Spending five hours every day on your phone is an unconscious defense mechanism to avoid confronting your real life. The screen acts as an anaesthetic that drowns out your daily anxiety while actively destroying the cognitive vitality needed to act. Every hour lost to passive digital consumption reinforces your cowardice and postpones the inevitable confrontation with reality.

Freedom Cannot Be Begged For

You want the freedom to choose your own life path while demanding a guarantee that nobody around you feels hurt. True autonomy demands accepting the discomfort of being labeled disobedient or selfish by people bound to traditional scripts. Waiting for parental permission to live an authentic, conscious life is a childish illusion that guarantees lifelong compromise.

What to do

Five steps for you

Step 1

Reclaim Mental Energy from Digital Numbing

Why: Passive screen addiction depletes your cognitive capacity and masks the urgency of your life situation.

How: Place your phone in another room during non-working hours and replace mindless scrolling with silence and deep study.

Step 2

Draw a Firm Boundary on Arranged Marriage

Why: Entering a marriage to soothe family anxiety is an act of violence against yourself and an innocent partner.

How: Tell your parents calmly and unequivocally that you will not entertain any marriage proposals for the next three years.

Step 3

Separate Essential Support from Traditional Vanity

Why: Genuine love cares for real bodily and medical needs, not the preservation of traditional social prestige.

How: Calculate the exact amount needed for your parents essential living expenses and refuse to fund social display.

Step 4

Conduct Factual Reality Tests in Writing and Teaching

Why: Romanticizing alternative careers from a distance prevents you from testing whether you have genuine competence.

How: Dedicate ten hours every week outside your engineering job to actually write essays or teach students for free.

Step 5

Observe Internal Guilt Without Reacting

Why: Guilt is the primary weapon used by social conditioning to force compliance from conscious individuals.

How: When parents use emotional blackmail, sit quietly and observe the physical sensation of guilt without apologizing or capitulating.

Watch out

Mistakes to avoid

⛔ Marrying to Buy Temporary Domestic Peace

Using another human being as a shield to satisfy family pressure turns companionship into an exhausting, commercial cage.

⛔ Quitting Your Job in an Emotional Tantrum

Abruptly walking away without discipline or financial runway merely substitutes corporate stress for financial panic.

⛔ Engaging in Endless Arguments with Parents

Trying to convince conditioned elders through philosophical debate is futile and drains your energy.

⛔ Treating Writing and Teaching as Spiritual Rescues

Projecting your longing for psychological peace onto a romanticized creative profession is a complete category error.

Your plan

Your 90-day plan

Days 1-30
  • Cut daily phone screen time strictly below two hours by locking non-essential apps
  • Establish a non-negotiable morning routine of physical exercise and reading serious philosophical literature
  • Audit your personal finances to define your exact monthly survival expenses and parental support budget
Days 31-60
  • Hold a direct, calm conversation with your parents setting an absolute boundary against arranged marriage
  • Begin teaching or writing ten hours a week during evenings and weekends to test your true competence
  • Build a liquid emergency fund representing twelve months of basic survival runway
Days 61-90
  • Evaluate the factual output of your writing and teaching work without romantic self-flattery
  • Refuse any family demands for money that serve social status or religious rituals rather than real needs
  • Make a conscious, fact-based decision to either pivot careers or master your current employment with detachment
