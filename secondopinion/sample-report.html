One more car to consider, and mistakes to avoid

Worth a look

Skoda Kushaq Signature 1.0 TSI automatic (2026)

Our research library suggests it as a better fit: 5-star Global NCAP crash safety, a smooth torque-converter automatic (8-speed on the 2026 model) that doesn’t overheat in crawling traffic, and a stable chassis without much body roll.

Ex-showroom about ₹15.7 lakh. The manual is about ₹17.2 lakh on-road in Mumbai, so expect the automatic to cost a little more; get a Pune quote.

Common mistakes with these cars

  1. Paying ₹18–20 lakh for a non-turbo CVT/IVT engine just for features, instead of a punchier, safe powertrain
  2. Assuming the top Creta turbo can be had for ₹18 lakh, when tax and registration push it to about ₹24 lakh
  3. Buying the Curvv for its styling without test-driving its body sway and braking
