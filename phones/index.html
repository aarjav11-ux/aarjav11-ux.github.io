Phone myths, busted

The popular choice isn’t always the right one for you. Here’s what to know first.

Myth 1

“Zyada megapixel matlab best camera”

Sach: Photo quality depends on sensor size, aperture and image processing, not just the megapixel number.

Myth 2

“Curved display hamesha premium hota hai”

Sach: Curved screens look good but break more easily and are harder to protect. A flat screen is often more practical.

Myth 3

“Badi battery matlab do din pakka”

Sach: An inefficient chipset or poor software can drain even a big battery quickly. Efficiency matters as much as size.

Myth 4

“Sale mein hi sabse sasta milta hai”

Sach: Prices sometimes rise before a sale. Check price history and compare specs before you buy.

Myth 5

“Shopkeeper jo bole wahi best”

Sach: Stores often push the phones with the highest margin. Compare online and offline before deciding.

Myth 6

“Saare 5G phones same hote hain”

Sach: If a phone lacks the 5G bands your carrier uses, you won’t get a true 5G experience. Band support matters.
