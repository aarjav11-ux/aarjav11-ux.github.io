Salesmen ke jhooth se bacho

Appliance myths, busted

The popular choice isn’t always the right one for you. Here’s what to know first.

Myth 1

“Saare 1.5 ton AC ek jaisa cool karte hain”

Sach: Cooling capacity varies between models of the same tonnage. Check the actual cooling capacity in kW, not just the label.

Myth 2

“Har ghar ko 5-star AC hi lena chahiye”

Sach: If you use the AC only a few months a year, a 3-star model can be more cost-effective. Do the maths for your usage.

Myth 3

“Indian bartan dishwasher mein saaf nahi hote”

Sach: Steel utensils clean well in a dishwasher. Only some materials like raw aluminium, cast iron and brass should stay out.

Myth 4

“BLDC fan sirf remote se chalta hai”

Sach: Many BLDC fans also work with regular wall regulators, so a remote isn’t always needed.

Myth 5

“AC mein har saal gas bharwani padti hai”

Sach: An AC is a sealed system. Gas only runs out if there’s a leak, so a routine yearly refill is often unnecessary.

Myth 6

“AC ka filter kaafi hai, purifier luxury hai”

Sach: Basic AC filters don’t capture fine PM 2.5 particles. For bad air, a dedicated True HEPA purifier is what works.
