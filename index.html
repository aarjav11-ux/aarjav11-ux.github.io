Bhed chaal mein mat phasna

Showroom myths, busted

The most popular choice isn’t always the right one for you. Here’s what buyers should know before they sit down to sign.

Myth 1

“Top model hi lena chahiye”

Sach: Mid variants often share the same engine and safety structure. Pay for features you’ll actually use, not for the badge.

Myth 2

“Saare automatics same hote hain”

Sach: AMT, CVT, torque converter and DCT feel very different in traffic. The right one depends on how and where you drive.

Myth 3

“Dealer accessories pack zaroori hai”

Sach: Chrome kits and bundled add-ons inflate the on-road bill. Buy only what you need, and compare prices outside.

Myth 4

“Coating aur PPF lagwa hi lo”

Sach: For an everyday family car, expensive dealer paint treatments are rarely worth the markup. Decide calmly, not at the delivery desk.

Myth 5

“Waiting bahut hai, abhi book karo”

Sach: Long waiting periods are sometimes used to push stock or colours. Ask other dealers and check alternatives before paying a booking amount.

Myth 6

“Insurance dealer se hi lena padega”

Sach: You can compare insurance quotes online. The dealer’s package is often not the best price.

Questions buyers ask us

Is the report really personalised?

Yes. Every report is built from your own answers: your budget, city, driving pattern, family and must-haves. Each question carries a weight, so the things you care about most shape the result most.

Why 100 questions? That sounds like a lot.

Because a car is a big, long-term purchase and small details (like daily traffic, parking or who rides with you) change the right answer. The form is split into 14 short steps, and most people finish in 10–12 minutes.

Do you sell cars or work for a dealer?

No. We don’t sell cars. The report recommends cars based on your answers and our research, and it tells you honestly when a car is over your budget or misses one of your priorities.

Will you recommend a car that’s over my budget?

Only if nothing inside your budget meets your most important needs, and then we say so clearly, with the real on-road price and the gap.

How do I receive the report?

As a PDF on the email address you enter at the start of the questionnaire.

What happens to my data?

Your answers are used only to prepare your report. Your contact details are used only to send it to you.
